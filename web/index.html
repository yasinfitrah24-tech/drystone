Drystone

Know before you stack.

No mortar. Just placement.

A camera reads the box stack, a physics simulation estimates the chance of a fall, and fixed rules decide.

Why it matters: in 2020, US transportation and warehousing had 99,800 injuries with days away from work; 12,100 of them were “struck by object or equipment”. That category is broader than falling stacks. Source: US Bureau of Labor Statistics
